Kenya TOURISM

The Most top five Best Places In Kenya

mount kenya

Location: Central Kenya.
Best Time to Visit: January-February or July-September.
Info: Africa’s second-highest peak, ideal for hiking and exploring diverse ecosystems.

Lake Nakuru National Park kenya

Location: Rift Valley.
Best Time to Visit: June-March.
Info: A sanctuary for flamingos and rhinos, surrounded by picturesque landscapes.

Diani Beach

Location: Southeastern Kenya.
Best Time to Visit: December-March.
Info: A pristine beach with white sands, turquoise waters, and vibrant marine life.

Maasai Mara National Reserve

Location: Southwest Kenya.
Best Time to Visit: July-October (Great Migration).
Info: A wildlife-rich savannah famous for the Great Migration and the Big Five.
