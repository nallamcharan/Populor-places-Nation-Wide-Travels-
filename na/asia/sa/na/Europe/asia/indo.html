The Most top five Best Places In The Indonasia

Bali's Uluwatu Temple

Location: Bali
Best Time to Visit: April-October.
Info: A cultural hub known for rice terraces, temples, and yoga retreats.

Borobudar Temple

Location: Central Java.
Best Time to Visit:May-September.
Info: The world’s largest Buddhist temple, a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Komodo Island

Location: East Nusa Tenggara.
Best Time to Visit: April-December.
Info: Home to the Komodo dragon and pristine diving spots.

Mount Bormo

Location: East Java.
Best Time to Visit: May-August.
Info: An active volcano offering surreal sunrise views and crater treks.

Lake-Toba

Location: North Sumatra.
Best Time to Visit: May-September.
Info: A stunning volcanic lake with an island at its center, ideal for relaxation.
