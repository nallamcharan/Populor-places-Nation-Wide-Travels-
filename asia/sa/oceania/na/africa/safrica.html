Table Mountain

Location: Cape Town.
Best Time to Visit: October-April.
Info: An iconic flat-topped mountain offering panoramic views, hiking trails, and cable car rides.

Kruger-National-Park

Location: Northeastern South Africa.
Best Time to Visit: May-September.
Info: One of Africa's largest game reserves, known for the Big Five and exceptional wildlife safaris.

Robben-Island

Location: Cape Town.
Best Time to Visit: October-April.
Info: A UNESCO World Heritage Site, significant as the prison where Nelson Mandela was held.

Blyde River Canyon

Location: Mpumalanga.
Best Time to Visit: May-September.
Info: A lush canyon with dramatic cliffs, waterfalls, and scenic hiking trails
